Rooftop Garden

A Garden in the Sky

The GreenSkyRise rooftop garden helps absorb carbon dioxide, improve air quality, lower building temperatures, support local pollinators, and create a relaxing green space for all residents.

🌫️

Reduces carbon footprint

Native plantings absorb CO₂ and filter urban air pollutants daily

💨

Produces cleaner air

Over 200 plant varieties filtering particulates and releasing oxygen

🌡️

Natural cooling effect

Reduces rooftop temperature by up to 40°F, cutting A/C energy costs

🐝

Supports biodiversity

Certified pollinator habitat with seasonal wildflowers and bee gardens

🥦

Community gardening

Resident vegetable plots, herb gardens, and composting stations
